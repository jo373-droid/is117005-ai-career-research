# AI Operations and Automation Engineer

**Role family:** workflow automation · AI application operations · internal tooling

## What the role does

Finds repeatable work that can be safely improved with AI or automation, builds the workflow and integrations, tests the results, and supports the system after launch. The job may sit in IT, business operations, engineering, or a central AI enablement team.

## Real posting example

[Junior AI and Automation Engineer, Myriad360](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/myriad360/jobs/8930382002) is an early-career posting describing work with internal AI apps and automations: configure agents and prompts, connect applications through APIs and approved tools, prepare knowledge sources, test and monitor workflows, investigate failures, support users, and document procedures. It asks for 0–2 years and accepts coursework, internships, and personal projects. The posting says U.S.-based and includes a New York City pay-transparency range, but it does not name a NY/NJ worksite.

A separate indexed NYC posting titled [AI Operations Engineer at Conduct](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/conduct/844a86d5-dcda-4bdb-b19d-2c53d4010a30) described embedding AI in engineering, finance, sales, and operations. Its page did not load during this research pass, so confirm the posting is active before using it as an open vacancy.

## Skills to investigate

Python or scripting, APIs and JSON, workflow tools, process mapping, testing, logging and monitoring, troubleshooting, documentation, user support, and privacy/access-control basics. More technical versions may add cloud, data pipelines, RAG, agent orchestration, and software deployment.

## Career routes

IT support, business systems, QA, operations analysis, automation, or junior software roles can build relevant experience. Create a portfolio project that maps an existing workflow, defines where human approval is needed, measures failure cases, and documents security and support procedures.

## Research distinction

An “AI Operations Engineer” title does not always mean model operations (MLOps). Some postings focus on internal business processes; MLOps and AI platform postings focus on deploying, monitoring, and maintaining models and serving infrastructure. Read the duties before grouping them.
