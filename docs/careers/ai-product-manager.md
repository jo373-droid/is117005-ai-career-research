# AI Product Manager

**Role family:** product management · AI product development

## What the role does

Connects user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility to guide an AI product from problem definition through launch and iteration. The role typically coordinates rather than personally trains models.

## Common responsibilities to verify in postings

- Define the user problem and product outcomes.
- Work with engineering, design, data, legal, and operations teams.
- Decide where AI is appropriate and how success will be measured.
- Plan evaluation, feedback, and human review for AI features.
- Communicate limitations and manage product risks.

## Skills to investigate

Product discovery, prioritization, communication, data literacy, experimentation, and enough AI knowledge to discuss model capabilities and limits. Requirements vary; some employers expect prior product experience.

## Career routes

Product coordinator, business analyst, UX research, software, data, or domain-specialist experience can lead toward product roles. Build evidence through shipped projects, user research, and measurable outcomes.

## Evidence status

“AI Product Manager” is a title used in the market, but the title itself is not a standardized occupation with one growth rate. This profile is a research hypothesis to validate against current job postings.
