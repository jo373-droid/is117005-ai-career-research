# AI / Machine Learning Specialist

**Role family:** AI engineering · machine learning · applied research

## What the role does

Builds or adapts models and AI-enabled systems to solve a defined problem. Depending on the employer, the work may include preparing data, selecting methods, training or integrating models, evaluating quality, and supporting deployment.

## Common responsibilities to verify in postings

- Prepare datasets and establish evaluation criteria.
- Train, fine-tune, or integrate machine-learning models.
- Measure performance, reliability, and failure cases.
- Work with software and data teams to put models into production.
- Document limitations and monitor model behavior after release.

## Skills to investigate

Programming (often Python), statistics, data handling, machine-learning concepts, testing, version control, and clear communication. Production roles may emphasize cloud platforms, APIs, and MLOps; research roles may require deeper mathematics or graduate study.

## Career routes

Computer science, data science, engineering, or mathematics study; internships or project work; then junior ML, software, or data roles. Compare degree requirements carefully: some applied roles accept a bachelor's degree and demonstrable projects, while research roles often ask for advanced study.

## Growth context

The World Economic Forum identifies AI and machine learning specialists among the fastest-growing roles expected by surveyed employers through 2030. This is a global employer survey outlook; it does not guarantee openings in a particular location. [Source](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook)
