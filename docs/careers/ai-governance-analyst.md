# AI Governance Analyst

**Role family:** responsible AI · risk · compliance · policy operations

## What the role does

Helps an organization understand and manage how AI systems are selected, documented, evaluated, and monitored. The work can sit in risk, privacy, legal, compliance, data governance, or responsible-AI teams.

## Common responsibilities to verify in postings

- Maintain inventories and documentation for AI systems.
- Coordinate risk assessments and review processes.
- Track testing, approvals, and mitigations.
- Translate policy or standards into operational guidance.
- Help teams communicate system limits and accountability.

## Skills to investigate

Research and documentation, risk analysis, data and AI literacy, stakeholder coordination, privacy or compliance knowledge, and concise writing. Technical depth and legal expertise vary by employer.

## Career routes

Relevant foundations include information systems, data governance, cybersecurity, public policy, privacy, audit, or compliance. Adjacent analyst and risk roles may be entry points.
