# Data Scientist

**Role family:** data science · analytics · applied statistics

## What the role does

Uses data, statistics, and computational methods to answer questions, make predictions, or support decisions. AI may be part of the toolkit, but many data-science roles focus on experimentation, forecasting, measurement, and communicating evidence.

## Common responsibilities to verify in postings

- Gather, clean, and analyze data.
- Build statistical or machine-learning models where useful.
- Design experiments or evaluate interventions.
- Explain uncertainty and results to nontechnical partners.
- Turn analysis into recommendations or usable data products.

## Skills to investigate

Statistics, SQL, Python or R, data visualization, experimental design, and written communication. Employers may also request cloud data tools, domain knowledge, or machine-learning experience.

## Career routes

A bachelor's degree in statistics, computer science, information systems, economics, or a related field can be a starting point. Analytics, research, and data analyst roles can provide a bridge; project work should show both technical analysis and clear explanation.

## Growth context

BLS projects U.S. data scientist employment to grow **33.5% from 2024 to 2034**, adding about 82,500 jobs. This is an occupational projection, not a forecast limited to AI-branded positions. [Source](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm)
