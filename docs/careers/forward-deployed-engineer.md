# Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

**Role family:** applied AI · software engineering · customer deployment

## What the role does

Works directly with a customer or operating team to understand a real workflow, then builds and integrates software that solves it. AI-focused FDEs may develop LLM and agent applications, retrieval systems, evaluation tools, and production integrations. They often stay close to deployment, reliability, and user feedback.

## Real NY examples

- [Associate Forward Deployed Engineer, Provectus](https://jobs.lever.co/provectus/62394851-693e-47fe-a86c-f8d9acf43ec2): New York, three days onsite, explicitly asks for 0–2 years and describes pairing with a senior engineer to learn client work and ship production code.
- [Forward Deployed AI Engineer, SAP](https://careers.sap.com/job/New-York-Forward-Deployed-AI-Engineer-NY-10001/1421516033/?feedId=384233): New York hybrid, specifies 3–7 years and independent ownership of customer-facing AI workstreams, cloud deployment, and production operations.

## Skills to investigate

Strong software fundamentals, Python or TypeScript, APIs, cloud, debugging, and the ability to communicate with technical and nontechnical users. AI deployments may add LLMs, RAG, agents, evaluations, observability, and security controls.

## Career routes

An early-career route can begin through software, data, cloud, or application engineering plus internships and projects that integrate AI. The Associate FDE example explicitly accepts internship, co-op, research, or project experience. More senior roles expect independent customer delivery and production engineering experience.

## What makes it different

The distinguishing feature is being close to the customer’s workflow and owning implementation in the field. FDE roles can include product feedback, integration, prototyping, and deployment; compare each posting to see how much is software engineering versus consulting or sales support.
