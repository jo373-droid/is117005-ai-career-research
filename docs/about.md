# About this project

**Course:** IS 117-005  
**Project:** Research portfolio on new job titles, job descriptions, and career options in fast-growing areas of AI technology.  
**Research snapshot:** November 2026

## Purpose

This portfolio organizes early research into understandable career profiles. It is designed to grow as sources, job postings, and career requirements are investigated.

## How to read it

Growth figures are labeled by source and method. Government projections, employer surveys, and job posting counts answer different questions and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Research practice

Each profile should be updated with dated evidence, clearly linked sources, and a note about geography and methodology. The pages are educational research, not a guarantee of job availability or a substitute for checking current postings.
