# AI job market snapshot: New York & New Jersey

**Checked:** November 5, 2026  
**Scope:** Publicly visible employer postings and employer internship pages with New York City or New Jersey locations. Postings change quickly; this is a dated research sample, not a promise that each opening will remain available.

## What counts as an “AI job” here?

AI work shows up under more than one kind of title. Some employers hire people who build models; others need people who connect AI to products and enterprise systems, run the infrastructure, automate internal work, or secure and govern AI deployments. These role families overlap, and titles are not standardized.

| Role family | Titles seen in postings | Typical work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applied AI / model engineering | AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, AI/ML Engineer | Build AI features; integrate models, retrieval, tools, and data; evaluate quality; put systems into production |
| Customer deployment | Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), Forward Deployed AI Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer | Work directly with a client to understand a workflow, build a working system, integrate enterprise services, and support production delivery |
| AI platform / infrastructure | AI Infrastructure Engineer, AI Platform Engineer, MLOps Engineer, AI Engineer (MLOps) | Operate model-serving and agent infrastructure, cloud and GPU environments, deployment pipelines, monitoring, reliability, and cost controls |
| Workflow and business operations | AI Operations Engineer, Junior AI & Automation Engineer, AI Automation Engineer | Find repeatable internal workflows; configure agents and integrations; test outputs; monitor automation failures; document and support users |
| Data and research | Data Scientist, Machine Learning Research Intern, Data Science & System Security Research Intern | Analyze data, train or evaluate models, build datasets and benchmarks, or research systems and security questions |
| Security and assurance | Senior Security Engineer, AI Security; AI Red Team Engineer; AI Risk & Governance Lead | Threat-model AI products, set technical guardrails, test misuse and exposure risks, document controls, and coordinate responsible deployment |

**Early pattern:** “New” titles often combine existing disciplines. An AI infrastructure role is still infrastructure and reliability engineering with model-serving or GPU systems; a Forward Deployed Engineer combines software delivery and customer problem-solving; an AI operations role can be automation engineering plus workflow discovery and support. The job description matters more than the title.

## How the evidence maps to BLS occupations

Many AI titles do not appear as distinct occupations in government statistics. BLS’s AI/IT discussion instead tracks established occupational groups that can include AI work: data scientists, information security analysts, software developers, computer and information research scientists, actuaries, and operations research analysts. For 2024–2034, BLS projects U.S. employment growth of 33.5% for data scientists, 28.5% for information security analysts, and 15.8% for software developers. These figures describe the broader occupations; they are **not** AI-only job growth rates. [BLS, “Artificial intelligence, information technology, and employment, 2024–34”](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm)

The World Economic Forum’s 2025 report separately records employer expectations through 2030 and lists AI and machine learning specialists and big data specialists among fast-growing roles. It is a global survey outlook, not a U.S. vacancy count or BLS projection. [WEF, Jobs Outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook)

## What this sample suggests for students

1. **Build a shared foundation:** programming, data handling, APIs, cloud basics, Git, troubleshooting, and written communication appear across several paths.
2. **Choose a problem domain:** business workflow automation, cybersecurity, financial services, life sciences, or customer deployment can differentiate an AI skill set.
3. **Show working evidence:** internships and associate FDE roles explicitly value projects, research, and the ability to explain what failed and how it was improved.
4. **Treat security and operations as AI career paths:** AI systems need access controls, data protection, monitoring, evaluation, support, and incident response after a model is selected.
5. **Track actual openings separately from long-term outlook:** save a dated record of title, location, level, source URL, posted/checked dates, duties, minimum qualifications, and application status.
